Quadrealm Quest
An exciting tactical board game where players must outmaneuver and outsmart their opponents in a battle for survival.

Game Objective:
Be the last player with at least one character standing by eliminating all other players.

Setup:
Board Setup:
The board is divided into 8 sides, with 4 sides containing 3 different lanes and a central realm, and 4 sides that have no realms.
Players choose a starting side and place their characters in the designated starting area.
Character Setup:
Each player selects 3 characters, each with their own unique attributes:
Health: Fixed health pool for each character.
Attacks:
Basic: Default attack (lower energy cost).
Skill: More powerful attack (higher energy cost).
Ultimate: Most powerful attack (highest energy cost).
Energy: Players start with 0 energy and gain 1 energy at the start of each turn.
Your HP is the sum of your characters HP. You die, when your HP becomes 0. 
Card Setup:
Common Deck: Contains various action and support cards that players can draw.
Each player draws 1 card from the common deck at the start of each turn.
Each player has 6 card slots, and they can discard a card to gain 1 energy.
Players can equip 2 support cards in their support slots.
Support Cards: Special abilities that players can equip, with 2 available slots.
Event Cards: Can be used at any time and discarded after use (e.g., increased damage or reduced energy costs).

Gameplay:
Turn Order:
Players take turns in clockwise order, performing the following phases:
Energy Phase:
Gain 1 energy at the start of your turn.
Draw Phase:
Draw 1 card from the deck to add to your hand (you can have up to 6 cards in hand).
Movement Phase:
Roll Dice: Roll a dice to determine how far your active character can move along the board.
Land on Spaces: Depending on where you land, different effects may occur:
Realm Effects: Unique to each terrain type (e.g., extra damage, healing, etc.).
Event Spaces: Trigger special event effects.
Energy Cost for Movement: Some spaces require specific energy types to traverse (e.g., movement costs 1 energy for certain terrains).
Choosing Lanes: When entering a realm, choose between 3 lanes:
Top Lane: Costs 1 energy.
Middle Lane: No cost.
Bottom Lane: Provides 1 energy.
Energy Management (Optional):
Discard 1 card from your hand to gain 1 energy.
Support Card Management (Optional):
Play any support cards from your equipped support slots.
Discard used support cards.
Event Card Management (Optional):
Play any event cards from your hand.
Action Phase:
Switch Characters (Optional): You can switch your active character. Note that you cannot attack in the same turn you switch characters.
Attack (Optional): Choose an enemy character to attack.
Choose the type of attack (Basic, Skill, Ultimate).
Expend the required energy for the attack.
Resolve the attack, considering any modifiers based on terrain, abilities, and character types.

Victory Condition:
Eliminate all other players by reducing all of their characters’ health to 0. The last player with characters remaining wins.

Character Cards:
Long Range Attacker:
Archer (20 HP)
Simple Arrow: Deal 2 damage to the target. Energy cost: 0.
Heavy Arrow: Deal 4 damage to the target. Energy cost: 2.
Exploding Arrow: Deal 4 damage to the target and 2 damage to all other enemies in the same realm (Area of Effect). Energy cost: 4.
Ballista Engineer (20 HP)
Bolt Launcher: Deal 2 damage to the target. Energy cost: 0.
Piercing Spear: Deal 3 damage to all enemies in the same terrain as the target. Energy cost: 3.
Tracking Radar: Deal 5 damage to the target. If the target moves during the turn, deal an additional 3 damage. Energy cost: 4.
Close Range Attacker:
Knight (30 HP)
Thrusting Sword: Deal 2 damage to the target. Energy cost: 0.
Heavy Blow: Deal 5 damage to the target. Energy cost: 2.
Excalibur Strike: Deal 7 damage to the target. Energy cost: 3.
Samurai (25 HP)
Quick Slash: Deal 2 damage to the target. Energy cost: 0.
Shadow Step: Deal 4 damage to the target and prevent them from moving during their next turn. Energy cost: 2.
Warrior’s Stand: Deal 9 damage to the target and prevent them from moving during their next turn. Energy cost: 4.
Mage:
Cleric (15 HP)
Water Ball: Deal 2 damage to the target. Energy cost: 0.
Misty Fog: Deal 2 damage to the target and heal 2 health to all allies in the same terrain. Energy cost: 3.
Potion Blast: Heal 6 health to an ally. Energy cost: 4.
Necromancer (20 HP)
Spirit Orb: Deal 2 damage to the target. Energy cost: 0.
Spirit Drain: Steal 2 health from each opponent in the same terrain. Energy cost: 4.
Dark Resurrection: Steal 5 health from the target. The Necromancer cannot move during the next turn. Energy cost: 5.
Heavy Build:
Berserker (30 HP)
Stone Throw: Deal 2 damage to the target. Energy cost: 0.
Rampage: Deal 4 damage to the target and 1 damage to yourself. Energy cost: 2.
Sacrificial Rampage: Deal 15 damage to the target and 50% of your remaining health as self-damage. Energy cost: 5.
Titan Warlord (35 HP)
Battle Cry: Deal 3 damage to the target and 1 self-damage. Energy cost: 0.
Devastating Blow: Deal 6 damage to the target and 2 self-damage. Energy cost: 2.
Apocalyptic Slam: Deal damage to the target equal to 3 times the total energy used in this turn. The Titan Warlord takes self-damage equal to the energy used. Energy cost: All remaining energy.
Neutral Build:	
Gambler (25 HP)
All or Nothing: Deal 3 damage to the target. If the roll is 1, 2, or 3, the Gambler deals 0 damage. Energy cost: 0.
Game of Dice: Deal damage to the target equal to the dice roll. The damage is halved if the roll is 2, 4, or 6. Energy cost: Dice Roll / 2.
Fate’s Hand: If the dice roll is 5 or 6, deal 10 damage to the target and draw 2 cards. Energy cost: 4.
Tactician (20 HP)
Reinforcement Order: Draw 1 card from the deck. Energy cost: 0.
Chaos Swap: Swap the positions of two characters on the board. Energy cost: 2.
Landmine: Deal 5 damage to all opponents within 4 tiles. Energy cost: 3.

Terrains and Their Effects:
Mountain (Favored: Archer, Berserker)
Eagle’s View: Archers gain +1 AOE damage on attacks.
Relentless Assault: Berserkers gain a free skill use when using skill/ultimate.
Forest (Favored: Archer, Swordsman)
Natural Cover: Archers take -2 damage from incoming attacks.
Forager’s Resilience: Swordsmen regenerate 2 HP at the start of each turn.
Volcano (Favored: Mage, Swordsman)
Molten Power: Mages convert healing into damage at a 2:3 ratio.
Heated Edge: Swordsmen gain +2 damage to all attacks.
Hot Spring (Favored: Mage, Berserker)
Rejuvenating Waters: Mages receive +1 bonus healing from all sources.
Unyielding Fury: Berserkers ignore self-inflicted damage.

Game End:
The game ends when all opponents have been eliminated (all of their characters have 0 HP).
The last remaining player with at least one character standing is the winner!
