Game Objective:
Elimination: Be the last player with at least one character with remaining health.
Setup:
Board: We have 8 sided board, with 4 sides having 3 different lanes, and a common realms, and 4 sides without any realms
Characters: Each player starts with 3 characters, each with unique:
Health: A fixed health pool.
Attacks:
Basic: Default attack, often with lower energy cost.
Skill: More powerful attack with higher energy cost.
Ultimate: Most powerful attack with highest energy cost and potential special effects.
Energy: Each player starts 0 energy, and gains 1 energy at the start of each turn.
Deck: We have a common draw pile, and each player draws a card at the start of a turn.
Support Cards: Can be equibbed by a player, if they have any empty support slots. Each player has 2 support slots. A support card can be discarded whenever a player needs to. 
Event Cards: can be played immediately, and then moves to the discard pile(e.g., Increased Damage, Reduced Energy Costs).
A person can hold 6 cards in hand.
A player can discard a card and gain an energy
Gameplay:
Turn Order: Players take turns in a clockwise order.
Pick an energy
Draw a card from the decke correc
Movement Phase:
Roll the dice and move your active character along the board.
Land on spaces with potential effects:
Realm Effects: Have specific effects depending on the player type.
Event Spaces: Trigger special events 
Energy Cost for Movement: Some spaces or terrain types may require the expenditure of specific energy types to traverse.
At the start of each realm, player has to choose a lane. Top lane costs 1 energy, middle row has no cost, and bottom row gives the player 1 energy
Energy Management (Optional):
Discard a card from your hand to gain one energy
Support Card Management (Optional):
Use a support card from your play area.
Discard used support cards.
Event Card Management (Optional):
Use a Event card from your hand
Action Phase:
Switch Characters (Optional): Switch your active character. You cannot attack after switching.
Attack (Optional):
Choose an enemy character to attack.
Expend the required energy based on the chosen attack (Basic, Skill, Ultimate).
Resolve the attack, considering character types and any relevant modifiers.
Victory Condition:
Eliminate all other players by reducing all of their characters' health to 0.

Terrains and Their Effects

1. Mountain (Favored: Archer, Berserker)

Eagle's View: Archers gain enhanced visibility, causing their attacks to deal +1 AOE damage.

Relentless Assault: When a Berserker uses a skill or ultimate, they gain a free additional skill use.



2. Forest (Favored: Archer, Swordsman)

Natural Cover: Archers take -2 damage from all incoming attacks due to the dense foliage.

Forager’s Resilience: Swordsmen regenerate 2 HP at the start of each turn.



3. Volcano (Favored: Mage, Swordsman)

Molten Power: Mages convert healing effects into damage at a 2:3 ratio.

Heated Edge: Swordsmen gain +2 damage to all attacks due to the volcanic heat tempering their strikes.



4. Hot Spring (Favored: Mage, Berserker)

Rejuvenating Waters: Mages receive +1 bonus healing from all sources.

Unyielding Fury: Berserkers ignore self-inflicted damage while fighting in this terrain.
